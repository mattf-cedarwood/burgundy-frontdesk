Late checkout

Two tiers. Offer it to every guest at check-in, not at checkout.

Standard checkout is 11 AM Late Checkout $50 Room until 1:00 PM — two extra hours. Cloudbeds item · SKU 900013
The one worth asking for Later Checkout $77 Room until 7:00 PM — the whole day. Cloudbeds item · SKU 900014
Price is the price

$50 and $77 are what the guest pays. No tax is added to either one. Don't quote a different number, and don't discount.

Before you sell it, check the room

You can only promise a late checkout if the room is actually free that long. Two questions, in this order:

1. Is another guest arriving into that same room today?

NOSell it. Nothing else to check.
YESGo to question 2.
↓

2. Is there another room of the same room type open tonight?

YESSell it — then move the arriving guest into that open room so housekeeping isn't stuck.
NODon't sell the late tier. Offer 1 PM if it works, otherwise a normal checkout.
7 PM on a sold-out night

If the hotel is sold out, the $77 tier needs GM approval before you promise it. Ask first.

What to say

Say it while they're standing at the desk with their card out — not the morning they leave.

At check-in

“Checkout is 11. If you'd rather not rush out, I can hold the room until 1 for $50 — or keep it all the way to 7 tonight for $77, so you can ski or sit by the fire and shower before you drive.”

The 7 PM tier sells itself in Big Bear because the drive home is the problem, not the room. Lead with what it solves: one more run, one more soak, a shower before a two-hour drive.

How to charge it

Same five steps for a late checkout or a can of Coke.

First time only

If your folio screen shows a green ADD/ADJUST CHARGE button instead of a blue + Add button, click Switch to the new folio in the blue banner at the top. These steps use the new folio.

  1. Open the reservation and click the Folio tab

    Search the guest's name or room number, open the reservation, then click Folio.

  2. Click + Add, then Product

    Not Room Revenue. Not Adjustment. Product — that's the one that pulls from our item list.

  3. Pick the room and the category

    Room # fills in from the reservation. Set Product category first — it filters the list so you're not scrolling past 80 items.

    Use Fee for late checkout. Use Beverages, Beer or Snacks for anything from the cooler.

  4. Choose the product — the price fills itself

    Click Product name and start typing. Type checkout for the late tiers, or the drink name for anything else. Never type the price by hand. If the price doesn't fill in on its own, you picked the wrong item.

    Selling three beers? Change Quantity to 3. Don't add the item three times.

  5. Set payment method, then Add

    Taking a card now? Choose the card. Putting it on the room? Leave Do not collect payment and it lands on their folio for checkout. Then click Add.

Why this matters

Every charge is stamped with the name of whoever was logged in. Ringing a drink through Pet Fee or Firewood because it was faster means the sale doesn't show up as yours, and the item counts don't tell us what to reorder.

Singles, six packs and buckets are separate items

A Budweiser single is $4. The six pack is $15 — it is not six singles. The pool bucket is $20. Pick the one the guest is actually getting; the pricing is already built to reward the bigger order.

Sales tax

Food and drink carry 7.75% California sales tax, added by Cloudbeds automatically. The shelf price and the guest's total are both in the list below — quote the shelf price and let the system do the math.

Check yourself

Answer before you open it. If you miss one, reread that section.

A guest checks in Friday. Housekeeping says someone is arriving into that exact room Saturday. Can you sell the $77 checkout for Saturday?

Only if another room of the same room type is open Saturday night, so the arriving guest can be moved. If nothing of that type is free, you can't sell it — offer 1 PM instead.

How much tax goes on a $77 later checkout?

None. $77 is the full price. Sales tax applies to food and drink, not to checkout fees.

A guest wants six Budweisers. What do you ring up?

One Budweiser Lager (6 Pack) at $15. Not six singles at $4 — that would charge them $24 for something priced at $15.

The price field is blank after you picked a product. What went wrong?

You haven't actually selected an item from the dropdown, or you picked one with no price set. Reselect from the list. Never type a price in by hand.

It's 4 PM, a guest wants two Topo Chicos, and you're slammed. Why not just add $8 to Firewood?

Because that sale disappears. It doesn't get credited to you, the drink count stays wrong so we reorder the wrong things, and the tax posts against the wrong item. Two clicks in the Beverages category and it's done right.

When is the best moment to offer late checkout?

At check-in, while they're at the desk. By checkout morning they've already packed and the answer is almost always no.
